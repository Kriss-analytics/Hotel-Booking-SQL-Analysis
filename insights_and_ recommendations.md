# Hotel Booking Analysis - Insights & Recommendations

## Project Summary

This document summarizes the key business insights and recommendations obtained from the SQL analysis of the Hotel Booking dataset. 
The analysis answers ten business questions designed to help hotel management understand customer behavior, revenue generation, 
booking patterns, cancellation trends, and seasonal demand.

---

## Question 1

### Business Question

Which market segment generates the highest revenue?

### Insight

Online Travel Agents (Online TA) generated the highest revenue, contributing approximately 20,463,144 in total revenue. 
This indicates that Online TA is the hotel's most profitable market segment, making it a key driver of overall revenue 
performance.

### Business Recommendation

1. Strengthen partnerships with Online Travel Agencies to maintain high booking volumes.
2. Invest in targeted digital marketing campaigns aimed at travelers who book through online platforms.

## Question 2

### Business Question

What is the average booking lead time?

### Insight
The analysis shows that the average booking lead time is 80 days, indicating that guests typically make their reservations
nearly three months in advance. This booking pattern provides the hotel with sufficient time to forecast demand, optimize room availability,
and implement strategic pricing before guests arrive.

### Business Recommendation

The hotel should leverage this 80-day booking window by introducing early-bird promotions and applying dynamic pricing 
strategies to maximize revenue. Additionally,management can use this advance booking period to improve staffing plans, 
inventory management, and operational resource allocation during both peak and off-peak seasons.

## Question 3

### Business Question

Which Room type have the highest cancellation rates?

### Insight

The analysis revealed that Room Type H has the highest cancellation rate at 41%. This may indicate pricing sensitivity or mismatch between expectations and room allocation for this category. The hotel may need to review pricing, overbooking policies, or customer targeting for this room type.

### Business Recommendation

The hotel should investigate the factors contributing to the high cancellation rate for Room Type H by reviewing its pricing strategy, room allocation process, cancellation policies, and customer feedback. Additionally, management should consider implementing targeted promotions or flexible booking options to reduce cancellations and improve occupancy for this room category.

## Question 4

### Business Question

How many Bookings were made by Market Segment?

### Insight

The analysis shows that the Online Travel Agent (Online TA) market segment recorded the highest number of bookings, with 55,941 bookings. This indicates that Online TA is the hotel's primary booking channel and plays a significant role in driving occupancy levels and overall booking volume.

### Business Recommendation

The hotel should continue to strengthen its partnerships with Online Travel Agencies to sustain booking volumes while also investing in direct booking channels through loyalty programs, exclusive discounts, and targeted marketing campaigns. Diversifying booking sources can help reduce dependence on third-party platforms and improve profit margins by lowering commission costs.

## Question 5

### Business Question

What is the distribution of bookings across Customer Types?

### Insight

The analysis reveals that Transient customers account for the largest share of hotel bookings, representing 75.03% of all bookings. This indicates that the hotel primarily caters to individual travelers rather than groups or contract-based customers, making transient guests the hotel's dominant customer segment.

### Business Recommendation

The hotel should continue to enhance the experience of transient guests by offering personalized promotions, loyalty programs, and flexible booking options to encourage repeat visits. At the same time, management should explore opportunities to increase bookings from group and corporate customers to diversify its customer base and reduce reliance on a single customer segment.

## Question 6

### Business Question

Which Room types generates the highest Revenue?

### Insight

The analysis shows that Room Type A generates the highest revenue, contributing approximately 14,505,528 in total revenue. This indicates that Room Type A is the hotel's most profitable room category, driven by strong customer demand, high occupancy, and an effective pricing strategy.

### Business Recommendation

The hotel should continue to promote Room Type A through targeted marketing campaigns and maintain its pricing strategy to maximize revenue. Additionally, management should analyze the key factors behind its strong performance and apply similar pricing, promotional, or service strategies to other room categories to improve their revenue potential.

## Question 7

### Business Question

During which Season is Revenue Highest?

### Insight

The analysis indicates that Summer is the highest revenue-generating season, contributing approximately 25,796,254 in total revenue. This demonstrates a strong increase in customer demand during the summer months, making it the hotel's peak revenue season and a critical period for maximizing profitability.

### Business Recommendation

The hotel should capitalize on the increased demand during the summer season by implementing dynamic pricing strategies, offering premium room packages, and ensuring adequate staffing and operational resources to maintain service quality. Additionally, targeted promotional campaigns can be introduced during lower-demand seasons to help balance occupancy and stabilize revenue throughout the year.

## Question 8

### Business Question

Which Countries have most Bookings?

### Insight

The analysis reveals that Portugal recorded the highest number of bookings, with 47,899 bookings. This indicates that Portugal is the hotel's largest source market, contributing significantly to overall occupancy. The strong demand from this market highlights its importance to the hotel's business performance and growth.

### Business Recommendation

The hotel should continue to strengthen its presence in the Portuguese market by implementing targeted marketing campaigns, loyalty programs, and personalized promotions to encourage repeat bookings. Additionally, management should identify and develop strategies to attract guests from other high-potential international markets, reducing dependence on a single country while expanding the hotel's customer base.

## Question 9

### Business Question

What is the Ratio of Repeat Customers vs New Customers?

### Insight

The analysis shows that only 2.96% of bookings are from repeat customers, while 97.04% are from new customers. This indicates that the hotel attracts a high volume of first-time guests but has a relatively low customer retention rate. The findings suggest an opportunity to improve customer loyalty and encourage repeat visits through targeted retention strategies.

### Business Recommendation

The hotel should implement customer loyalty programs, personalized promotions, and exclusive discounts for returning guests to increase repeat bookings. Additionally, improving guest satisfaction through exceptional customer service and post-stay engagement can strengthen customer relationships, enhance retention, and reduce the cost of acquiring new customers.

## Question 10

### Business Question

What are the monthly trends in booking numbers?

### Insight

The analysis shows that booking activity increases significantly during August, September, and October, indicating a period of high customer demand. In contrast, booking volumes decline in November, December, and January, reflecting lower seasonal demand. These trends highlight clear fluctuations in booking patterns throughout the year and emphasize the impact of seasonality on hotel occupancy.

### Business Recommendation

The hotel should capitalize on the high-demand months by implementing dynamic pricing strategies, offering premium packages, and ensuring adequate staffing to deliver excellent customer service. During the low-demand months, management should introduce promotional offers, discounted room rates, and targeted marketing campaigns to stimulate demand, improve occupancy, and maintain a steady revenue stream throughout the year.

# Overall Conclusion

The SQL analysis of the Hotel Booking dataset provided valuable insights into customer behavior, booking patterns, revenue performance, and market trends. The findings revealed that Online Travel Agents (Online TA) are the hotel's primary source of both bookings and revenue, while Room Type A is the most profitable room category. The analysis also showed that guests typically book 80 days in advance, with Summer emerging as the highest revenue-generating season.

Furthermore, the results highlighted important business opportunities, including the high cancellation rate for Room Type H, the hotel's heavy reliance on Transient customers and the Portuguese market, and the relatively low percentage of repeat customers. These findings suggest that while the hotel performs well in attracting new guests and maximizing seasonal demand, there is significant potential to improve customer retention, diversify booking channels, reduce cancellations, and increase revenue during low-demand periods.

Overall, the insights generated from this analysis can support data-driven decision-making in pricing, marketing, customer relationship management, and operational planning, ultimately helping the hotel improve profitability, occupancy, and long-term business performance.
