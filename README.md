# Hotel Booking Analysis using SQL

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a hotel booking dataset using SQL to uncover insights into customer booking behavior, revenue performance, cancellation patterns, and seasonal trends. The objective is to answer key business questions that can help hotel management make data-driven decisions to improve profitability and operational efficiency.

## Business Objectives

The objectives of this project were to answer the following business questions:

1. Which market segment generates the highest revenue?
2. What is the average booking lead time?
3. Which room types have the highest cancellation rates?
4. How many bookings were made per market segment?
5. What is the distribution of bookings across customer types?
6. Which room types generate the highest revenue?
7. During which seasons is the revenue highest?
8. Which countries have the most bookings?
9. What is the ratio of repeat customers versus new customers?
10. What are the monthly trends in booking numbers?

## Dataset

The dataset contains hotel booking records, including customer information, booking dates, room types, market segments, countries, lead time, cancellation status, and pricing information.

The dataset was used to analyze booking behavior, customer trends, revenue performance, and seasonal demand.

## Key Insights

- Online Travel Agents generated the highest revenue.
- Room Type A was the most profitable room category.
- Guests booked an average of 80 days before arrival.
- Summer generated the highest hotel revenue.
- Portugal recorded the highest number of bookings.
- Only 2.96% of bookings came from repeat customers.

## Business Recommendations

- Strengthen partnerships with Online Travel Agencies.
- Improve customer loyalty through reward programs.
- Reduce cancellations for Room Type H.
- Implement dynamic pricing during peak seasons.
- Increase marketing efforts during low-demand months.
- Diversify booking channels to reduce dependency on one market segment.

## Conclusion

The analysis of the Hotel Booking dataset provided valuable insights into revenue performance, customer behavior, booking patterns, cancellations, and seasonal demand. The findings show that Online Travel Agents are a major driver of bookings and revenue, while Room Type A generates the highest revenue.

The analysis also identified opportunities to improve business performance, including reducing cancellations, increasing repeat customer bookings, strengthening high-performing markets, optimizing pricing during peak seasons, and stimulating demand during lower-demand periods.

Overall, the project demonstrates how SQL can be used to transform hotel booking data into actionable business insights that support data-driven decision-making and improve revenue and customer retention.
